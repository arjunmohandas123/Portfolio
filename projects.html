01
Robotic Vacuum Cleaner with SLAM & ROS
Mini Project · Mar – Jun 2023 · APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University
IEEE Published ICKECS 2024

A fully autonomous ROS/SLAM-based vacuum robot built on Raspberry Pi. Achieved reliable map coverage over a 40 m² test environment with less than 5% drift error. Research was peer-reviewed and published at IEEE ICKECS 2024.

  • Implemented GMapping SLAM achieving reliable map coverage over 40 m² with <5% drift error
  • Developed sensor fusion pipeline combining LiDAR and wheel odometry with PID-based closed-loop motor control
  • Enabled real-time obstacle avoidance operating at a 5 Hz navigation cycle
  • Simulated and validated in both Gazebo and real-world environments

02
Medical Humanoid Robot
B.Tech Final Year Project · Aug 2023 – May 2024
Final Year Project AI Voice Interaction

Designed and built a full humanoid robot with a dual-controller embedded architecture (Raspberry Pi + Arduino) for real-time multi-layer hardware coordination. Demonstrated at a university exhibition with positive faculty evaluation.

  • Integrated 12 servo actuators, IMU and force sensors with a dual-controller embedded architecture
  • Developed sensor-feedback control achieving stable dynamic balance during coordinated limb movement
  • Integrated AI-based voice interaction module for patient-facing communication
  • Full system integration, hardware testing, and validation pipeline
